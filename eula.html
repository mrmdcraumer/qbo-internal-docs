End-user license agreement

Effective October 26, 2026

This agreement governs authorized internal use of Integrate, provided by Morgan Records Management LLC ("Morgan"). It does not replace a client's service agreement, Business Associate Agreement or other signed contract.

1. License and permitted users

Morgan grants its authorized employees and approved contractors a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use Integrate for Morgan's internal business purposes, subject to their assigned permissions and company policies. Access is not offered for public registration, resale, distribution or use for an unrelated business.

2. What Integrate does

Integrate supports sales leads, scope and quotes, agreements, project handoff, staff tasks, communications and billing. Specific features depend on the user's role and the workflows Morgan has enabled.

When enabled and authorized, the QuickBooks Online connection links or creates practice customers, creates and sends agreement deposit invoices, and reads invoice and payment records to reconcile billing and update project work. The current connection does not process card charges, bank debits or refunds. It does not replace Accounting's responsibility to review the books.

3. Authorization and responsibility

  • Use your assigned account and permissions. Protect credentials and report suspected unauthorized access promptly.
  • Connect only a QuickBooks company you are authorized to connect. Review the company and environment before granting Intuit consent.
  • Review customer identity, billing information, recipients, amounts and approvals before initiating a transaction or communication. Do not bypass required reviews or controls.
  • Use sandbox testing for fictional transactions. Confirm the environment before working with real records.
  • Use information only for authorized company work. Do not copy, disclose or export it without permission.

4. Data and confidentiality

The Integrate privacy policy describes information processed by the application and its accounting connection. Company confidentiality and security requirements continue to apply. QuickBooks-bound billing fields must not contain patient lists, clinical information or medical-record attachments.

5. Third-party services

QuickBooks Online, Intuit, e-signature, hosting, identity and email services remain subject to their own applicable terms and availability. Integrate is operated by Morgan, not by Intuit. Permission to use Integrate does not grant rights to another service or authorize transactions outside your role.

6. Availability and disclaimer

Integrate is provided "as is" and "as available," without warranties to the extent permitted by law. Morgan does not promise uninterrupted service or error-free results. Users must report errors and verify important business outcomes. Any limitation of liability applies only to the extent permitted by applicable law and does not override obligations that cannot lawfully be excluded.

7. Access changes and termination

Morgan may modify, suspend or revoke access in accordance with company policy. When authorization ends, you must stop using the application. An authorized QuickBooks administrator may disconnect the accounting connection. Disconnecting does not cancel invoices or erase existing company records.

8. Updates and contact

Morgan may update this agreement as Integrate changes. The effective date identifies the current version. Questions or support requests may be sent to dcraumer@morganrecords.com.
