Disconnect QuickBooks

Updated October 26, 2026

Opening this page does not disconnect anything. An authorized administrator must complete the steps below. This page does not confirm whether a connection is active.

Revoke access in QuickBooks Online

  1. Sign in to the intended QuickBooks company as a company administrator or another user authorized by QuickBooks to manage the connection.
  2. Open Integrations → Manage integrations (some layouts use My integrations or Apps).
  3. Find the Integrate connection. The existing developer/test app is named Integrate Sandbox; confirm both the app and company before proceeding.
  4. Open its action menu, choose Disconnect, and complete QuickBooks' confirmation prompts.
  5. Check that QuickBooks shows the connection removed or disconnected, and notify Morgan's Integrate support so pending work can be reviewed.

For current screen guidance, see Intuit's instructions for managing connected integrations.

What disconnecting changes

Revoking consent prevents further API access under that connection. An operation already accepted by QuickBooks may still exist or finish there. Disconnecting does not cancel an invoice, refund a payment, remove an existing customer, or erase historical records in either system. Accounting should review unfinished invoices and reconciliation work before reconnecting or changing companies.

Pausing an Integrate workflow or removing a practice's billing setup is different from revoking the company's Intuit authorization. For a full disconnection, use QuickBooks' disconnect action above or ask support to help revoke the connection.

Help and data requests

Contact dcraumer@morganrecords.com with the company name, whether this concerns sandbox or production, and the issue. Support will verify your authority before taking action. Do not include passwords, authorization codes or tokens.

See the privacy policy for retention, correction and deletion requests. An email request or visit to this page alone is not confirmation that access has been revoked.
