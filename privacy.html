Privacy policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Operator: Morgan Records Management LLC ("Morgan," "we," "us"). This policy covers Integrate, Morgan's internal business workspace, its authorized client interactions and its QuickBooks Online connection. It does not replace any applicable signed confidentiality agreement, Business Associate Agreement or notice governing a separate medical-records service.

1. Information we process

  • Account and access information: staff names, business email addresses, identity-provider identifiers, roles, permissions and sign-in/session information.
  • Business workflow information: practice and client contacts, addresses, scope, quotes, contracts, signatures and related evidence, project tasks, attachments, forms and communications submitted through authorized workflows.
  • Restricted operational information: authorized records-management workflows may contain patient lists, patient details or medical-record attachments. Access to this information follows the applicable role restrictions and confidentiality obligations. It is separate from the practice billing information sent to QuickBooks.
  • QuickBooks accounting information: the authorized company's identifier; customer names, contact and billing details; configured item and tax references; invoice identifiers, dates, amounts, balances and delivery status; and payment identifiers, dates, amounts, references and links to invoices. We retain relevant provider responses and reconciliation evidence to associate accounting activity with the correct practice and agreement.
  • Connection and audit information: OAuth authorization credentials, connection status, user actions, approvals, timestamps, request identifiers, errors and delivery/reconciliation receipts.

Information comes from authorized users, clients submitting information to an approved workflow, Morgan's business systems and service providers, and the QuickBooks company authorized through Intuit. Integrate uses OAuth authorization rather than collecting a user's QuickBooks password.

Practice billing, not patient billing. The QuickBooks connection is for Morgan's practice-level accounting. Do not put patient names, clinical information, patient lists or medical-record attachments in QuickBooks-bound fields. The current connection does not request the QuickBooks Payments API scope or collect payment-card credentials.

2. How information is used

We use this information to provide authorized sales, agreement, project and billing workflows; identify the correct customer; create and deliver approved deposit invoices; reconcile payments already recorded in QuickBooks; release eligible project work; send related notices; maintain audit evidence; troubleshoot failures; and protect access to company systems.

The QuickBooks consent grants Accounting API access. Integrate uses that access for its enabled accounting workflows; it does not use QuickBooks data for advertising or sell it. Morgan processes this information for its own business operations, not on Intuit's behalf.

3. Access and service providers

Information is available to authorized Morgan personnel and approved contractors according to their duties and permissions. Service providers process information needed to operate the relevant feature, including cloud hosting, identity, e-signature and email delivery. QuickBooks receives the customer and invoice information needed for accounting and invoice delivery. Intended recipients receive the forms, documents and notices addressed to them.

We may disclose information when required by law or when necessary to investigate a security incident or protect lawful rights. QuickBooks and other third-party services also apply their own terms and privacy policies. See Intuit's Global Privacy Statement.

4. Safeguards

Integrate uses authenticated access, role-based permissions, encrypted HTTPS connections to its web interface and external APIs, and server-side storage of integration credentials with restricted access. Connection credentials are not published on these documentation pages. Audit records support investigation and reconciliation. No system can guarantee absolute security; report suspected exposure promptly using the contact below.

5. Storage and retention

Integrate retains business records, accounting links, provider evidence and audit history as needed for the authorized workflow, accounting and reconciliation, security, and applicable recordkeeping obligations. Retention depends on the record's purpose and applicable obligations; this policy does not promise a fixed deletion period.

Disconnecting QuickBooks revokes future API access. It does not automatically delete invoices or payments in QuickBooks, Integrate's existing business records, or required audit evidence. Information no longer needed for an authorized purpose or required retention is eligible for secure deletion. Contact us to request review of retained information, including copies in backups.

6. Choices and requests

An authorized administrator can disconnect QuickBooks. Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held in Integrate, or to ask about retention or an integration. Requests are handled by authorized Morgan staff, who verify the requester's identity and authority and assess the request under applicable law and recordkeeping obligations. If some information must be retained, we will explain the applicable limitation. Requests concerning Intuit's own account records must also be directed to Intuit.

7. Public documentation and browser storage

These static documentation pages do not include forms, advertising trackers or analytics scripts. They are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process visitor request information under its privacy statement. The authenticated Integrate application uses secure session cookies for sign-in, and browser session and local storage for preferences and recovery of unfinished work.

8. Changes and contact

The effective date identifies the current version of this policy. We will update this page when Integrate's information practices change.

Privacy and support requests: Dan Craumer, dcraumer@morganrecords.com. Do not send passwords, access tokens, payment-card details or patient records by ordinary email.
