Policies and support

Updated October 26, 2026

Integrate is Morgan Records Management LLC's internal workspace for sales, agreements, project handoff, operational work and billing. Access is limited to authorized employees and approved contractors. Clients may receive specific forms, documents, signing invitations or notices through separately authorized workflows.

Public documentation; private application. These pages explain the application and its policies. They do not provide access to Integrate, company records or a QuickBooks company.

QuickBooks Online connection

Integrate's accounting connection supports practice-level customer setup, agreement deposit invoices, invoice delivery and reconciliation of payments already recorded in QuickBooks. Verified payment information updates the related project workflow and notifications.

Features run only when configured and enabled by Morgan. A QuickBooks company administrator must authorize the appropriate company through Intuit's consent process. Sandbox testing and live accounting use separate connections.

The connection uses the QuickBooks Online Accounting API. It does not currently charge cards, debit bank accounts or issue refunds. Reading a recorded payment is different from moving money.
